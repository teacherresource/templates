    Note heads

    Shape notes give each step of the scale its own note head. Pick four shapes (fa sol la mi) or seven (do re mi fa sol la ti).

    Type into any line or space and it prints beside the staff. Empty boxes print nothing, so you can label only the ones you're teaching.

    Fill with

    A rhythm line has no lines and spaces to label.

    Prints small in the corner when "Print answers" is on, and appears in Present mode when you press A.

    Starter decks

    Loading a starter replaces the cards on screen. Undo brings them back.
    Add many cards at once

    Type one card per line. To add a heading, caption, answer or lyrics, put them after double slashes in that order:

    c4/q d e f // Measure 1 // caption // C D E F // do re mi fa

    New cards copy the staff setup of the card you're on. On a grand staff the notes go to the soprano part.
    How to write music on a card

    With the mouse

    Click the staffadds a note at that line or space, using the length picked in the Length row. Click between two notes to put one in the middle. Click a noteselects it. Drag it up or down to change the pitch, or use the buttons to change its length, sign, tie or fermata. Shift-clickstacks another note onto the nearest one to make a chord. Grand staffthe top staff takes soprano and alto, the bottom takes tenor and bass. Click a part's box first to choose which one you're writing.

    With a selected note

    ↑and ↓ move it a step. Hold Shift for an octave. ←and → step to the note before or after. Deleteremoves it. Esc lets go of it.

    By typing

    c4a note name and its octave. c4 is middle C c4/qadd a length: w whole, h half, q quarter, 8 eighth, 16 sixteenth c4/q.a dot after the length makes it dotted c4/8 d e flater notes keep the last octave and length, so this is four eighths f#4 bb3# sharp, b flat, n natural. Notes follow the key signature unless you add one r/qa rest of that length c4+e4+g4/hnotes joined with + stack into a chord |starts the next measure. || is a double bar |: … :|repeat signs c4/h~ c/qa ~ after a note ties it to the next one (c4 d e)a slur from the first note to the last {c4/8 d e}a triplet c5/h^a fermata x/qa note on a rhythm line, where pitch doesn't matter

    Lyrics

    Each syllable goes under the next note of the top part. Put hyphens inside a word (A-maz-ing), a lone _ to let a note pass without a syllable, and an underscore inside a syllable where you want a space (O_Lord). Rests and the second note of a tie are skipped for you.
